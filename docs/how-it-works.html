wit-ai-search · how it works

From a WordPress post to a cited answer in ChatGPT

Three stages: articles are synced into Postgres, split into passages and turned into vectors in the background, then searched live when someone asks ChatGPT a question. Pick a stage, then play it or step through it.

    Why chunks, not whole articles
    
      
    One vector for a 3,000-word article blurs every topic in it together. Passages of about 250 words, each starting with the article title, keep each vector about one thing. Search returns the best passage per article.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Why the same model twice
    
      
    Stored passages and your question are both embedded with gte-small inside Supabase. Vectors from different models can't be compared, so both sides use the same model by design.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Why keyword search too
    
      
    Small embedding models can miss exact names like “HotelQuickly” or “CAAC”. Postgres full-text search catches those, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges the two ranked lists into one.
